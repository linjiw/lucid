The scientific distinction is between loss of environmental support and loss of policy competence. A never-shrink rule treats the first. A quality-preserving learning intervention must treat the second. Previously admitted conditions require continued exposure and periodic checks.

## Related work and bounded novelty

DORAEMON already maximizes randomization entropy subject to a success constraint and discusses easy-region collapse, forgetting during backtracking, and a possible policy KL remedy. Constrained expansion or reference-policy regularization alone is therefore insufficient differentiation. [DORAEMON, Sections 4 and 6 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.01885v2).

PPF adds expert-action squared-error regularization to PPO and reduces its strength where a model-based expert's assumptions are unreliable. Its teacher and selection mechanism differ from the solved learned origin and retained-state buffer proposed here, but its preservation mechanism is close prior work. [PPF, Sections V-B–V-C](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09833v2).

TransCurriculum combines command, terrain, and dynamics curricula and uses empirical tracking thresholds for expansion; multidimensionality alone is not the contribution. [TransCurriculum, Sections III-C–III-F](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14156v1). Time-uniform confidence sequences provide an established repeated-look framework, with assumptions to instantiate rather than a guarantee obtained merely by citing them. [Howard et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240).

The proposed differentiation is the measured value of separately constrained recovery and retained tracking feedback, including useful abstention, beyond a strong protected fixed training recipe. It remains a hypothesis until the matched comparison succeeds.
